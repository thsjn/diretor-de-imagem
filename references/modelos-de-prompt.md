## Exemplo A: moda infantil, foto principal


**Arquivo:** `01-dupla-maos-dadas.png` · **Size:** `1024x1536`

```
Professional children's fashion catalog photograph for a Christmas collection. The clothing is the hero of the image.

SCENE DIRECTION: Two little girls walk slowly side by side across a mint studio set, holding hands between them, both turning their heads toward each other mid-laugh as if one just whispered a secret. Natural stride, one foot slightly lifted, skirts swinging softly with the step. Their joined hands sit at the exact center of the frame, drawing the eye to both dresses.

MODELS: Left: Brazilian girl of Afro-Brazilian descent, 6 years old, voluminous natural curly hair styled in two high puffs, rich deep brown skin with natural glow, big open smile showing baby teeth. Right: Latin American girl, 7 years old, light olive skin, long dark brown French braid tied with a pink satin bow, wearing a gold paper party crown slightly tilted, eyes half closed from laughing. Natural, unposed childlike energy, no makeup look.

WARDROBE (must be exact and fully visible): Left girl wears a white cotton dress with royal blue polka dots of varied sizes, square neckline, ruffle flutter straps, gathered skirt to mid-calf, hem border printed with large blue flowers with orange centers and small orange blossoms with green leaves. Right girl wears an emerald green cotton dress with a fine sparkle dot texture, wide flutter ruffle sleeves, square neckline, fitted bodice, gathered full skirt, hem border printed with whimsical red, pink and coral flowers, small red and white mushrooms and green foliage. Bare legs, simple white shoes. Garment colors true to life, fabric weave and gathering clearly readable.

SET: Seamless studio backdrop in soft mint teal (#7FCFC6), floor in the same tone with a smooth cove so there is no visible horizon line. Silver glitter paper stars of different sizes hang on invisible threads at varied heights and depths, some close to the backdrop, some closer to camera. Nothing overlaps the dresses.

CAMERA AND LENS: Full-frame mirrorless camera, 85mm prime lens at f/2.0, ISO 100, 1/320s, camera lowered to the girls' chest height, subject distance about 3.5 meters, vertical framing from just above the crown to just below the feet with breathing room.

DEPTH OF FIELD AND LENS BLUR: Focal plane locked on both girls' faces and the front of the dresses; both models sit on the same plane so both are tack sharp. Background falls off smoothly: stars hanging near the backdrop render as soft, creamy defocused shapes, their glitter turning into small round specular bokeh discs with smooth edges, no onion ring texture, no harsh outlines, slight cat-eye shape only at the frame corners. Backdrop texture dissolves into a clean even gradient. Gentle optical vignette, no digital blur artifacts, natural transition between sharp and soft zones.

LIGHTING: Large octabox key light high at 45 degrees camera left, white V-flat fill camera right, soft strip light behind each girl as hair rim to separate curls and braid from the mint backdrop, subtle background light creating a slightly brighter center glow behind the subjects. Bright high-key commercial look, soft natural contact shadows on the floor, catchlights in both eyes.

COLOR AND FINISH: Clean commercial e-commerce grading, accurate skin tones for both children, emerald green deep and saturated, whites clean without blue cast, mint backdrop calm and even. Fine natural film-like grain, extremely high detail, realistic hands with correct fingers.

STRICT: No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no captions, no graphic overlays, no borders. Full dresses visible, hems never cropped.
```

## Exemplo B: textura de pintura com luz rasante


```
Photorealistic close-up photograph of a real hand-painted flower pattern on watercolor paper, shot from a low side angle. Use the attached image only as reference for the colors and the flower pattern. The painting must look genuinely hand-painted with gouache and watercolor, NOT a digital print.

SCENE DIRECTION: The camera sits low at the edge of the desk, about 35 degrees above the surface, looking across the painted sheet toward the top. A child's hand rests on the paper border at the right, holding a brush lifted just off the paper, a drop of blue paint about to fall from the tip. The palette is visible in the background, soft.

THE PAINTING: Heavy cold-press watercolor paper, cream white border, lime yellow-green background wash (#DDF08F range) with visible brush streaks, uneven pigment, granulation in the paper grain, soft watermarks and hard drying edges. Periwinkle blue (#A9C1F5 range) six-petal daisies in three sizes painted in opaque gouache, each one slightly different, petals with rounded imperfect edges, raised pigment ridges and visible brush strokes, orange (#E8954A range) round centers dabbed thick with slight relief. Some petals slightly lighter where the paint is thinner. The paper is visibly cockled from water, forming soft gentle waves across the painted area.

CAMERA AND LENS: Full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/2.2, ISO 100, 1/200s. Horizontal framing.

DEPTH OF FIELD AND LENS BLUR: Focus on a cluster of flowers in the middle third, where texture, brush marks and paint relief are razor sharp. Flowers closer to the lens fall into soft foreground blur, flowers toward the far end and the palette melt into creamy defocused shapes. Natural optical fall-off along the plane of the paper.

LIGHTING: Low raking warm window light from the left, grazing across the paper so every bump of paper tooth, every gouache ridge and the waves of cockling cast tiny shadows and highlights. The wet brush tip and the drop of paint catch a bright specular highlight. Soft fill from a white card on the right.

COLOR AND FINISH: Soft cream and lime tones, periwinkle blue, warm orange, natural skin tone on the hand. Editorial lifestyle grading, fine natural grain, ultra high detail, realistic child fingers.

STRICT: No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no graphic overlays. Nothing vector-like or perfectly uniform.
```

## Exemplo E: regerar foto de catálogo trocando a criança (edit, testado no Codex)

Base: a foto antiga da página. Referência: a arte plana da estampa. Saiu com roupa idêntica, rosto novo e cor natural.

```
Professional children's fashion catalog photograph, Christmas collection, front view catalog shot. Photographic edit of a real studio photo. The clothing is the hero and must stay identical.

INPUT IMAGES:
Image 1 (<original photo>): EDIT BASE. Keep its exact pose, body position, hands, framing, crop, camera angle, set, hanging stars and the exact cut of every garment.
Image 2 (<print artwork>): flat artwork of the fabric print. Use it ONLY as the source of truth for print motifs, scale and colors. Ignore the paint palette, the hand, the brush, the paper and the logo in it.

MODEL:

The new face must clearly NOT resemble the child in Image 1: different face shape, eyes, nose, eyebrows, lips, jaw and cheekbones. Only the body pose, head turn, hairstyle type and accessories are kept from Image 1. Real child with natural child body proportions (head size proportional to the body), natural skin texture with fine pores and soft peach fuzz, real flyaway hairs, no makeup look, no plastic or airbrushed skin, no grid or pixel pattern artifacts, realistic hands with correct fingers.

WARDROBE (must be 100% identical to Image 1 in cut, length, ruffles, straps, neckline, bows and seams, and identical to the print artwork in motifs and colors):

Real woven cotton: visible weave, soft natural wrinkles, gathering at the waist seam. Print sits on the fabric and follows every fold. Do not invent, simplify, recolor or move print motifs.

SET AND CHRISTMAS MOOD:

## Exemplo F: logo para recorte

```

Image 1 (<crop of the original logo>): exact logo to recreate. Ignore its background and any props around it.
LOGO: <each line of text, size, alignment, letter style, color of each letter>. Spelling must be exact: <text with accents>.
